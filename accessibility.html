Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 1, 2026  ·  Morti Capital

Partially Conformant — WCAG 2.1 AA

Our Commitment

Morti Capital is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility. There are three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

Morti Capital is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard. We are actively working toward full conformance.

Measures Taken

Morti Capital takes the following measures to ensure accessibility:

  • Skip navigation link provided at the top of every page
  • All images include descriptive alternative text
  • All form inputs are associated with visible labels
  • Pages use proper heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
  • HTML lang attribute declared on all pages
  • ARIA landmark roles used for navigation, main content, and footer
  • Cookie consent banner is keyboard accessible
  • Keyboard focus is visible on all interactive elements
  • Color contrast meets or exceeds WCAG 2.1 AA on primary text
  • Motion-sensitive users: animations respect prefers-reduced-motion

Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some areas may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA:

  • Data visualizations and charts may not have full text equivalents for complex datasets
  • Third-party embedded content (charts, widgets) may not be fully within our control

We are working to address these limitations. If you encounter a barrier, please contact us.

Technical Specifications

Morti Capital relies on the following technologies for conformance with WCAG 2.1:

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript (ES6+)
  • WAI-ARIA

This site has been tested on Chrome, Firefox, and Safari with keyboard-only navigation and screen reader spot-checks (NVDA, VoiceOver).

Feedback & Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of Morti Capital. If you experience any barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us:

  • Email: hello@morti.capital
  • Response time: We aim to respond within 2 business days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the U.S. Department of Justice ADA Information Line at 1-800-514-0301.

Enforcement

If you are unhappy with how we have responded to your accessibility feedback, you may contact the relevant enforcement body in your jurisdiction. In the United States: the U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division, Disability Rights Section.

Formal Complaints

We are committed to resolving accessibility issues in good faith. Please contact us at hello@morti.capital before filing a formal complaint, as we are prepared to work collaboratively toward a solution.
