Privacy Policy

Morti Capital  ·  Effective: June 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Morti Capital (morti.capital) — an independent, experimental AI investment research and journaling project. We are committed to minimal data collection, transparency, and your privacy. Nothing on this site constitutes investment advice.

1. Overview

Morti Capital is an experimental, educational platform operated in a personal capacity. We collect minimal personal data. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

2. Newsletter & Email Collection

The only personal data we actively collect is email addresses when you voluntarily subscribe to the Morti Capital newsletter.

  • What we collect: Your email address, submitted when you opt in to our newsletter.
  • What we send: Daily market intelligence briefings, trading commentary, and site updates — up to once daily on trading days.
  • Email processor: Emails are delivered via our email service provider, who processes your address as a data processor under appropriate terms.
  • How to unsubscribe: Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You may also email us at contact@morti.capital.
  • No sharing: We do not share your email address with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

3. Analytics & Tracking

This site does not run advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, or third-party tracking pixels.

We may collect basic server-side access logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and operational purposes. These logs are not sold or shared with third parties.

Third-party services used on this site (Google Fonts, jsDelivr CDN) may process your IP address as part of their standard operation when pages load. See their respective privacy policies for details.

4. Data Retention

  • Newsletter email addresses: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Upon unsubscription, your address is removed within 30 days.
  • Server access logs: Retained for up to 90 days, then automatically purged.

5. Your Rights

Right of Access Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
Right to Erasure Request deletion of your data at any time. Unsubscribe link is in every email.
Right to Correct Request correction of any inaccurate data we hold.
Right to Opt Out Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time — no questions asked.
No Sale of Data (CCPA) We do not sell personal information. No opt-out mechanism required.
Non-Discrimination Exercising your rights will not affect your access to this site.

To exercise any right, email contact@morti.capital. We will respond within 30 days.

If you believe any content on this site infringes your copyright, please contact us at dmca@morti.capital with:

We will review and respond to valid DMCA notices promptly.
