Privacy Policy

Morti Capital  ·  Last updated: May 25, 2026  ·  Effective: May 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Morti Capital website (morticapital.com) and describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information. Morti Capital is an independent personal project operated in a personal capacity, unaffiliated with any employer. We are committed to compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and applicable global privacy standards.

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

Morti Capital is an independent, personal investment research and AI development project.

  • Project: Morti Capital (personal project, not a registered fund)
  • Website: morticapital.com
  • Privacy contact: contact@morticapital.com

For privacy-related requests, please email us. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal data. The only personal data we actively collect is email addresses submitted to our newsletter.

Newsletter Subscription:

  • Email address (when you voluntarily subscribe to receive the morning market brief)

Automatically Collected Technical Data:

When you visit this website, third-party services (described in Section 6) may automatically collect technical data including your IP address, browser type, and referring URL. We do not independently log or store server access data.

What we do NOT collect:

  • We do not collect names, payment information, or any sensitive personal information
  • We do not create user accounts or persistent profiles
  • We do not use behavioral tracking pixels or advertising identifiers
  • Portfolio and trading data displayed on this site is simulated (paper trading) and does not contain any visitor's personal financial information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use submitted email addresses exclusively to:

  • Deliver the morning market brief: Sending our daily pre-market intelligence email to subscribers who have opted in.
  • Service communications: Notifying subscribers of material changes to the newsletter or service.

We do not use your email address for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the newsletter service you subscribed to. We do not share email addresses with any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For individuals in the EEA or United Kingdom, we process your personal data under the following lawful basis:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): By submitting your email address to subscribe to our newsletter, you provide freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent to receive the Morti Capital morning brief. You may withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

5. Data Retention

We retain newsletter subscriber email addresses for as long as your subscription is active. If you unsubscribe, your email address will be deleted from our mailing list within 30 days. You may also request immediate deletion by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The following third-party services are used to operate this website:

  • Google Fonts: Typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Chart.js / jsDelivr CDN: A charting library is loaded from jsDelivr (a public CDN). jsDelivr may log your IP address as part of standard CDN operation. See jsDelivr's Privacy Policy.
  • Alpaca Markets (portfolio data): Live portfolio data is fetched server-side from Alpaca Markets' API. No visitor personal data is transmitted to Alpaca; the portfolio data displayed is solely about Morti Capital's simulated trading positions.
  • Email delivery service: Newsletter emails are delivered via a third-party email service provider. Subscriber email addresses are processed by this provider as a data processor under appropriate data processing terms.

7. International Data Transfers

Morti Capital operates from the United States. If you are in the EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction with data transfer restrictions, your email address may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

For EEA/UK subscribers, transfers are made under appropriate safeguards including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable. Our service providers maintain their own transfer compliance mechanisms. Contact us for details.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We do not set any first-party cookies and do not use cookies for analytics or advertising. We have no third-party advertising integrations on this site.

Third-party services (Google Fonts, jsDelivr) may set cookies or use browser fingerprinting as part of their standard operation. You can manage these through your browser settings. Because we do not use non-essential first-party cookies, no cookie consent banner has been implemented.

9. Your Rights

Right of Access (GDPR / CCPA) Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
Right to Erasure (GDPR / CCPA) Request deletion of your personal data. Unsubscribe link is in every email.
Right to Withdraw Consent (GDPR) Unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
Right to Rectification (GDPR) Request correction of inaccurate data we hold about you.
Right to Portability (GDPR) Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Right to Object (GDPR) Object to processing. For email: simply unsubscribe.
No Sale of Data (CCPA) We do not sell personal information. No opt-out mechanism required.
Non-Discrimination (CCPA) Exercising your rights will not affect your access to the newsletter.

To exercise any right, email contact@morticapital.com or use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter. We will respond within 30 days.

EEA/UK residents may also lodge a complaint with their national supervisory authority. For a list of EEA authorities, visit edpb.europa.eu. UK residents may contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the following additional rights:

  • Right to Know: Categories of personal information collected: Identifiers (email address). Source: directly from you. Purpose: newsletter delivery. Disclosure: only to email service provider as processor.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information at any time.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@morticapital.com.

11. Investment Disclaimer

Nothing on this website constitutes investment advice, a solicitation to buy or sell securities, or a guarantee of any financial outcome. All trading activity displayed is paper trading only (simulated capital — no real money is at risk). This site is for informational and experimental purposes only. Always consult a qualified financial professional before making investment decisions.

12. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or 16 for EEA/UK residents), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently received such data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Data Security

Email addresses submitted through our newsletter form are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. No transmission method is completely secure; we encourage you to be mindful of what information you share online.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by a new "Last updated" date. For active newsletter subscribers, we will send a notification email for material changes. Continued use of the site after updates constitutes acceptance.

15. Contact

  • Location: United States

We aim to respond to all privacy inquiries within 5 business days and to rights requests within the legally required timeframe.
